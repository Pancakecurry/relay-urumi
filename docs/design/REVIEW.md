Calm introduction, aligned labels, readable helper text and consistent full-width primary action. Existing browser acceptance exercises create/join onboarding and two separate sessions. Screenshots alone do not establish full WCAG compliance; exhaustive screen-reader/zoom/assistive-technology review remains C10.
